## Official surface identity

- Privora Command Center (PCC): server/root/reseller administration.
  - Default direct listener: 8443/TCP HTTPS.
  - Convenience path: `/pcc` redirects to the configured PCC listener.
- Privora Panel (PPanel): hosting-user administration.
  - Default direct listener: 2443/TCP HTTPS unless occupied.
  - Convenience path: `/ppanel` redirects to the configured PPanel listener.

The convenience paths redirect into the isolated control listeners; they do not mount the control application inside hosted-site PHP. PCC and PPanel retain independent session names and role admission rules.
